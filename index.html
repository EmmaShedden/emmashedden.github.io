About Me

I am a 3rd-year PhD student at the University of Pennsylvania, where I am fortunate to be advised by Rajeev Alur and Osbert Bastani. My research interests include formally guaranteed agentic security and neurosymbolic frameworks for robotic control. I'm grateful to be supported by the NSF GRFP.

I am a member of trustml@Penn and the ASSET Center.

In Fall 2026, I am a teaching assistant for CIS 4190/5190: Applied Machine Learning.

Previously, I completed my undergraduate degree in Computer Science and Mathematics in 2024 at the University of Michigan, where my research on CS pedagogy and cognition was advised by Wes Weimer. I also worked on feature conflict resolution in cyber-physical systems with Eunsuk Kang at Carnegie Mellon University, where I am an alumna of the REUSE program.
